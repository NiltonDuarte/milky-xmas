## Chicken Salpicão (Brazilian Chicken Salad)

**Servings:** 15
**Preparation time:** 40 minutes
**Difficulty:** Easy

### Ingredients
- 1 large chicken breast
- 1 can corn kernels, drained
- 1 can peas, drained
- 1 grated carrot
- 1 can (or carton) heavy cream/table cream
- Raisins, to taste
- Shoestring potato sticks (potato crisps), to taste
- Salt, to taste
- 1 chicken bouillon cube
- Fresh parsley, chopped
- Green onions (scallions), chopped
- Onion, finely chopped

*Source recipe from TudoGostoso.*

### Instructions
1. In a pot, add the chicken bouillon cube, a pinch of salt, and enough water to cook the chicken breast. Cook until fully done.
2. While the chicken is cooking, combine the corn, peas, grated carrot, and raisins in a large bowl. Season with salt, parsley, green onions, and onion.
3. Once the chicken is cooked, shred it and add it to the bowl with the other ingredients.
4. Stir in the cream and the shoestring potato sticks.
5. Transfer the mixture to a baking dish and bake for about 7 minutes.
6. Serve while still warm.

*Preparation steps translated from the original recipe.*

### Tips
- Add the cream last so it stays fresh-looking when serving.
- You can substitute the boiled chicken with roasted chicken for extra flavor.
- If you prefer, you can use mayonnaise instead of cream for a more traditional version.

*Tips based on the original recipe notes.*

**Note:** In Brazil, salpicão de frango is commonly served as a side dish at holiday meals, especially Christmas dinner, but it can also be enjoyed as a main dish.
