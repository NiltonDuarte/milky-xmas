# 🎄 How to fight the Grinch

**The Grinch is coming to steal Christmas! Here is how to stop him.**

🎅 **Join in** – Press **Start** when he appears. Don't worry if you're late: anyone can jump into a running fight just by attacking.

☃️ **Fight together** – Use the buttons under the message to hurt him. The more friends, the merrier!

🌲 **Watch out** – Sometimes he fights back. Pick wisely, or you'll feel the cold.

⏳ **Be quick** – The Grinch won't wait around. If time runs out, he wins.

🎁 **Surprises** – Stick around until the end. There may be goodies in it for you... and if he wins, there may be coal.

Good luck, and do your worst! 😈
