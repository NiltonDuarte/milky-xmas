## Decisions

- **Fixed damage per hit.** The product owner chose six hits for the whole group in each phase, so damage does not depend on the number of players. The numbers are the phase's HP divided by six, rounded up.
- **Heavy tile.** It stays a little stronger than the plain one, so some groups finish the attack phase in six hits or fewer.
- **Defense cap.** Small groups still need one defense per player. Larger ones stop at six.
