# Proposal

## Why

The final Grinch phase and the outcome of the fight should award items players can see in `/socks`.

## What Changes

- Add a snowflake to the final board. Each accepted pick credits one Snowball to the player.
- Award each participant one Coal and one Yellow Snowball per participant when the Grinch survives an ended fight.
- List Yellow Snowballs in `/socks`.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

- `grinch-inventory-rewards` covers item credits from final-board picks and losing fights.

### Modified Capabilities

## Impact

The core, shared encounter service, SQLite, and both adapters will change.
