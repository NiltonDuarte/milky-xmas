## Why

Mentions alone are hard to tell apart; the server wants to see each player's picture next to them.

## What Changes

- Wherever the bot mentions a player, their round avatar appears before the mention.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

- `player-avatars`: avatars next to mentions in Discord.

### Modified Capabilities

None.

## Impact

Discord only. The bot creates one application emoji per player from their avatar.
