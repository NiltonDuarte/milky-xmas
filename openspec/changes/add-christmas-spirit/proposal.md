## Why

At the end of the event the community should see what it did together: the Christmas spirit of the server.

## What Changes

- `/spirit` lets a moderator post the server's Christmas spirit: the good and bad deeds of all players added up, with a verdict and a picture.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

- `christmas-spirit`: the server-wide total of good and bad deeds.

### Modified Capabilities

None.

## Impact

A new command in both adapters, one read in the gift store, and one embedded picture.
