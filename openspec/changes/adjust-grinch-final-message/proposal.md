## Why

When the Grinch wins, the final message showed Max next to the Grinch's own picture, which dilutes the ending. The final leaderboards also listed every player, including those with nothing to show.

## What Changes

- When the Grinch escapes or defeats all players, the final message shows only Max's image.
- The damage, gift, and Snowball leaderboards list only players with points above zero. A leaderboard nobody scored on is left out.
- The damage leaderboard shows the damage dealt, the HP a player took from the Grinch, and not score points. A final hit removes 1 HP and awards 2 points, so the leaderboard now counts 1.
- Only the final attack awards points. The snowball, defense, and attack phases award none. The points are kept for each player for later use and no leaderboard shows them.
- This applies to the Discord message, the attached standings file, and the local chat.

## Impact

- Replaces the "Max image when the Grinch survives" requirement of `add-mod-give-gift-and-reply-formats` and adjusts the final standings of `rich-grinch-fight-message`.
- Player cards during the fight still show every player, with zero damage if they have none.
