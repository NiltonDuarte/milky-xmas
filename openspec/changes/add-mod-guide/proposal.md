## Why

Moderators need to know how the whole bot behaves and which numbers it uses, beyond what the player guide says.

## What Changes

- `/mod-guide` opens a private guide for moderators with a menu of topics: commands and permissions, setup, moderator commands, gifts, drops, aura, the Grinch, the tree, Advent, snowballs, quotes, saved data and restarts.
- The numbers in it are the ones the bot is using.
- `/help me-milky` lists it for moderators.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

- `mod-guide`: the moderators' guide to the bot.

### Modified Capabilities

None.

## Impact

A new command in both interfaces. Nothing is stored.
