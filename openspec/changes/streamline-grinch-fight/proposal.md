# Proposal

## Why

A Grinch fight should be readable without separate replies for successful clicks or changing boards.

## What Changes

- Make repeat Join clicks ineffective for the enrolled player.
- Update the fight board in place and acknowledge routine Discord clicks silently.
- Show the boss health as `👽 Grinch · <value>/100% HP`.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

- `grinch-fight-presentation` covers fight acknowledgements and health display.

### Modified Capabilities

## Impact

The Discord interaction adapter and web fight presentation will change.
