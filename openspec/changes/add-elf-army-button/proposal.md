## Why

The setup ends with the Christmas introduction in the Grinch channel. Players have no quick way to say they are in, or to be called together for a fight. An `elfs` role lets the community tag the whole army, and a button lets each player take it in one click.

## What Changes

- The Christmas introduction, posted when a moderator sets up a Grinch channel, carries a **Join the elf army 🛷** button.
- A click gives the player the `elfs` role. A click by someone who already has it changes nothing and says so.
- The bot creates the `elfs` role (mentionable) when it is missing, at setup and again on a click if needed.
- Only the introduction posted from now on has the button. Messages posted earlier do not change.

## Impact

- Discord only: the setup message, a new button handler, and the Manage Roles permission for the bot. The local chat has no roles.
- No core or storage change.
