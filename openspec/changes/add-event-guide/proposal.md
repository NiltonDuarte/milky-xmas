## Why

The event has many things to do and `/help me-milky` only names the commands. Players need a place that explains how each one works.

## What Changes

- `/guide` opens a private guide with a menu of topics covering everything a player can do.
- The numbers in the guide are the ones the bot is using.
- `/help me-milky` lists `/guide`.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

- `event-guide`: the player's guide to the event.

### Modified Capabilities

None.

## Impact

A new command in both interfaces. Nothing is stored.
