## Why

The fight shows the same picture in every phase. The developer supplied one picture for the Grinch's attack and one for the last 5%.

## What Changes

- While the players defend, the fight shows an animated picture of the Grinch.
- In the final attack, from 5% HP, it shows the Grinch with his heart.
- Other phases and the final messages keep their pictures.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

- `grinch-phase-pictures`: the picture of each running phase in Discord.

### Modified Capabilities

None.

## Impact

The Discord fight display and two embedded images. No rule changes.
