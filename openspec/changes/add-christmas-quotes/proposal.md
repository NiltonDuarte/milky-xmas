## Why

The channels should feel like Christmas while people chat, with a quote or a verse now and then.

## What Changes

- The bot posts a Christmas quote or poem in chosen channels, driven by the chat's activity.
- `/setup channel` gets an optional `quotes` choice.
- The tuning file gets a `quotes` section: messages needed and minimum time between quotes.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

- `christmas-quotes`: quotes posted from channel activity.

### Modified Capabilities

None.

## Impact

Channel settings, tuning, the Discord message handler, `/setup`, and the local chat's `/message`.
