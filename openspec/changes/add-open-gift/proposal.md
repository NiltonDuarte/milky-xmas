## Why

Gifts only serve to be sent. Players want something to do with the ones they keep.

## What Changes

- `/open gift <rarity>` spends one gift for a surprise that only the player sees.
- A shield blocks the Grinch's next hit, and an extra watering skips the wait of `/water` once.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

- `open-gift`: opening gifts and what comes out.

### Modified Capabilities

None. The fight and tree changes are not archived yet; these requirements add to them.

## Impact

A new command in both interfaces, `/water`, the Grinch fight, `/look in-your-stocking`, and two columns on the player's row.
