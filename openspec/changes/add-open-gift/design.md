## Decisions

- Normal: a Snowball, a Coal, or a Christmas quote. Rare: three Snowballs or an extra watering. Epic: a shield or two normal gifts. Choices of a rarity have the same chance.
- A shield and an extra watering are kept one at a time; a player who has it gets the other surprise.
- Opening does not move the aura, so keeping, opening, and sending are real choices.
- The shield works by itself on the next hit, so nothing has to be typed or pressed during a fight. It is taken in the same transaction that saves the fight.
- The extra watering is used only when the wait would refuse the watering.

## Risks / Trade-offs

- [An epic gift into two normal gifts feeds more openings] → It is a trade down in value.
